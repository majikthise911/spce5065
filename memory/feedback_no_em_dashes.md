**Zero em dashes. Ever.** In any document, submission, message, or file written for Jordan Clayton, do not use the em dash (—). He wants them completely gone, none anywhere. He also dislikes en dashes (–); use a plain hyphen (-) for ranges, or reword ("1 to 3").

Replace what an em dash would have done with: a colon, a comma, parentheses, a semicolon, or just split into two sentences. Pick whatever is grammatically cleanest for that spot.

**Check before delivering:** grep the output for `—` and `–` and confirm zero hits, including sneaky spots like document titles, section headers, image `alt` text, and table cells.

**Why:** He stated it flatly and with some frustration ("you have these em dashes all over the place and i do not want em dashes. zero. i want them all gone."), noting it should have been a standing instruction from the start. This overrides the older voice note that listed "em-dash asides" as a style trait. See [[feedback_submission_voice]].

**How to apply:** Write without em dashes in the first place, then verify with a grep pass. Applies to every artifact, not just homework submissions.
